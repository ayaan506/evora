Evora Staffing Terms & Guidelines
  • Payment Terms: Exactly 50% advance is required to confirm crew allocation. The balance 50% is due within 24 hours post-event.
  • Shift Overtime: Standard rates apply to planned hours. Any overtime exceeding 30 minutes is billed at standard hourly rates + 10%.
  • Staff Welfare: The client is requested to provide potable drinking water and basic staff meal arrangements for shifts exceeding 6 continuous hours.
  • Replacement Guarantee: In the rare event of staff illness or emergency, Evora guarantees on-site replacement within 60 minutes.
  • Cancellation Policy: 100% advance refund if cancelled 72+ hours before event start; 50% refund if cancelled within 24–72 hours.
